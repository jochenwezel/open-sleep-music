# Additional lullaby recording candidates

Fifty additional recording candidates extend the search for Lullabies for little ones beyond the earlier classical, Wikimedia Commons and Bandcamp selections. They include modern piano miniatures, acoustic guitar, lute, harp, celesta, music boxes and small ensembles. After feedback on 14 October 2026, 46 remain in the active selection: one listening favorite and 45 unreviewed candidates. Four rejected recordings are retained only in the exclusion record below.

These are research candidates, outside the runtime catalog and Preview collections. The sources declare compatible recording licenses, but `licenseReviewStatus` remains `unchecked` until the complete recording-specific admission review. The favorite records initial listening feedback; complete admission listening checks remain pending. An inviting title or a calming tag does not establish suitability for children.

The linked item pages identify the recordings and their declared licenses. Forty-eight direct originals returned valid MP3, Ogg or WAV signatures on 13 October 2026; this initial-byte check is not a complete decode or listening review. Two recordings are offered only inside archive packages and need member identification before offline delivery. Parenthesised lengths are source-provided values, not independently decoded durations. No upstream SHA-1 was supplied for these originals, so checksum fields remain empty.

The [research metadata](../tools/lullaby-research-2026-10-13.json) retains credits, source and license URLs, direct original links where available, probe results and per-recording review notes. Comparison with existing catalog and candidate source/download URLs found no overlap. Three separately titled recordings from MouthlessGames share one package page; alternate encodings or short edits are not counted as additional works.

## Recording rights and delivery notes

Kevin MacLeod's 27 ISRC-specific pages select the exact recording from the artist's [public track metadata](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/pieces.json) and render a title-specific CC BY 4.0 attribution block and original MP3 link. His [FAQ](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/faq.html) distinguishes the copyrighted recordings from public-domain compositions. The separate original MP3 delivery was checked for each selected ISRC. Do not substitute third-party reuploads.

Orange Free Sounds uses different licenses for different items. The five Alexander Blu pages above expressly declare CC BY 4.0 for their named recordings. Baby Music Box separately credits Thejack288 and links CC0, but original recordist provenance and the tune still require confirmation. Do not extend these grants to similarly titled uploads or other Orange Free Sounds pages.

Shane Ivers' two item pages identify him and declare CC BY 4.0. OpenGameArt entries retain their individual author credits and license links. Radakan credits Janne Hanhisuanto but was submitted by another account; keep uploader authority pending. For Forgotten Lullaby, the selected file is the explicitly supplied music-box loop, not the longer mix containing drums. For Manveru and Matthew Pablo, an archive is not an audio download URL.

Across the original 50-recording research, including the four exclusions, the sources declare 38 CC BY 4.0, six CC BY 3.0, two CC BY-SA 3.0 and four CC0 recordings. These declarations are research evidence, not production approval. Listen through the full originals for speech, noise, abrupt endings, intrusive peaks and unsuitable emotional tone. Check actual repetition boundaries for loops, and transitions between contrasting timbres. Keep instrumentation provisional where the source supplies only broad tags.
