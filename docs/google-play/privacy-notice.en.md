## What the app does

Open Sleep Music provides music and nature sounds for download and subsequent offline playback. You do not need an app user account. The app contains no advertising and no automatic usage analytics integrated by us. We do not create advertising profiles in the app or sell user data.

This notice describes Open Sleep Music. When you visit our website, the website processing described there also applies. General descriptions of web analytics and marketing in our [company privacy notice](https://www.compumaster.de/datenschutzhinweis/) do not mean that those functions are used in this app.

## Storage on your device

The app stores downloaded recordings, images and catalog information, as well as settings, favorites, blocked tracks and the last saved playback position, on your device. This enables offline playback and lets you resume using the app where you left off. This information is not automatically sent to CompuMaster as a usage report. Technical request and error data may be stored in local logs; these are not automatically transmitted to us either.

You can remove collections through library management. New downloads are stored persistently in the app’s own data area. The app does not need access to your personal music library for this. Versions released in 2026 attempt to move earlier downloads from the “Open Sleep Music” subfolder of the Music folder into this data area. Versions released from 2027 onward no longer access the previous Music folder. During migration, an old file is deleted only after the copy has been successfully verified.

On Android, the app’s own audio files are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Files that were not migrated from the old Music folder and previously shared reports may remain. The Android backup rules exclude downloaded audio files from automatic cloud backups and device transfers. Other app data, such as settings, may still be backed up by the operating system, depending on your device settings.

## Connections for catalogs, images and music

You decide which music recordings and collections to download. You can play recordings that have already been downloaded offline. When loading, however, the app automatically checks the catalog for updates; images are loaded as needed. These requests can also occur without a new music download. “Refresh library” also triggers a catalog request.

During these HTTPS connections, GitHub, as the delivery provider, receives in particular your IP address, the requested file, the time and technical request information. Audio recordings, images and catalogs are provided through GitHub; redirects within the associated delivery infrastructure may involve additional technical services. The app does not send its own persistent installation identifier, lists of favorites, playback history or support reports with these requests.

The original recording sources listed in the track information serve as provenance and license references. The app does not download the content from those source websites. The relevant website is opened in your browser only when you explicitly open a source or license link.

GitHub processes technical request data under its own [privacy notice](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). We have no direct access to the personal server logs held there and cannot delete them ourselves. Their retention is governed by GitHub’s purposes and rules.

Data may be processed outside the EU or the EEA, particularly in the United States. For transfers to GitHub, Inc. covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, we rely on the adequacy decision under Article 45 GDPR. According to the official register checked on 31 October 2026, GitHub is an active participant for non-HR data. Information about the provider and its certification is available in the linked privacy notice and in the [official DPF register](https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov/list).

## Voluntary feedback and support

Using “Share catalog feedback”, you can explicitly create a report, view a summary before sharing and then open an email draft addressed to appsupport@compumaster.de with the report attached. You review and send the message yourself in your email application. Without a suitable email application, no draft is opened and no report is sent. The report contains its creation time, app version, platform, catalog size, the tracks you have deliberately marked as favorites or blocked, and an optional comment. The app does not add a device identifier, playback history or local file paths.

For this handoff, a report file is stored in the local cache. Its cleanup follows cache management; it is not deleted immediately after the email application opens. Copies already handed over are subject to the storage practices of the respective application.

The report is not automatically sent to us. Your email provider and email application may process additional data and, in particular, add sender details. You control any subsequent changes in the email application. App support emails and feedback addressed to CompuMaster are processed through appsupport@compumaster.de. This does not cause the app to send anything automatically. If you send us feedback or a support request, we also process the contact details and content you provide in order to handle your request and, where appropriate, improve the selection of tracks. Please do not include health data or personal information about children in the comment.

Incoming requests are accessible to authorized employees and company management. Company email is processed through hosted services provided by IONOS. Personal feedback reports we receive are not forwarded to music providers.

Support requests and feedback are retained for as long as necessary to handle them, deal with required follow-up questions and document the matter. Where statutory retention obligations apply, or where a message is needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, further retention is governed by that reason. The type of document and the relevant starting date for the retention period are decisive; feedback is not all retained for the same length of time simply because it arrives by email. Once the reasons for retention no longer apply, personal content is deleted in accordance with the company’s email deletion policy. Backup copies expire within the associated backup cycle. Personal working copies that are no longer needed are deleted or depersonalized after evaluation. Findings about music selection may be retained without a link to the sender.

## Purposes and legal bases

Processing necessary for a requested app or support service is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR insofar as a contractual relationship with you exists or a corresponding pre-contractual request has been made. We base the technical requests necessary to deliver and update catalogs, images and selected recordings on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to provide app content that works, is up to date and is secure. Responding to general inquiries and improving the selection of tracks are also based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; our interests are to handle your feedback and provide a suitable music catalog. Legally required retention is based on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

Contact details and feedback are voluntary. Without the information necessary for your request, we may be unable to answer it. Offline playback does not require a support request. Technical connection data is necessary for online requests; no new content can be retrieved without a connection. We do not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects and do not carry out corresponding profiling.

## Children and families

The app is also intended for families with young children. Parents can select recordings and set up playback for their child. We need neither the child’s name nor their date of birth for playback. We do not use personalized advertising in the app.

As an adult caregiver, please take responsibility for downloads, opening external websites and sending feedback. Do not include health data or personal information about children in the comment. You can optionally enable child mode in the menu with a parent PIN and select the available collections. While child mode is locked, the app shows only approved collections with downloaded tracks and makes no internet requests of its own, even if the device is online. External links, feedback and collection management are locked. Parents can use the PIN to enable all functions again; leaving the app locks it again.

The child mode setting, collection selection and lockout after failed attempts are stored locally. A salted PIN verifier is held in the operating system’s protected storage; the PIN is not transmitted to us. When child mode is not enabled, or while the parent area is unlocked, the automatic catalog and image requests described above may occur. Child mode controls only this app, not the operating system’s own activities, the store or other apps.
