# Android privacy notice publication review

Status on 30 October 2026: revised publication candidate, not a recorded DSB
approval or authorization to publish. Public copy is in
[privacy-notice.de.md](privacy-notice.de.md). The website task must replace its
older copy, retain draft status, and verify the sitemap exclusion. No notice URL
has been submitted to Play as a final declaration by this change.

## Resolved drafting and operational points

- Scope is Android/Google Play; the unreviewed Apple section is omitted.
- Persistent private Audio storage, legacy-file limits and Android audio backup
  exclusions match `docs/audio-storage.md` and the source manifest/XML. Device
  restore tests remain app-release evidence, not public editorial placeholders.
- The maintainer explicitly confirmed the configured support mailbox, authorized
  handling and working-copy deletion/depersonalization on 30 October 2026. Original
  correspondence follows the central email concept, consistent with records
  017/008. No historical six-year blanket period is represented as approved.
- The candidate describes retention criteria, not invented numerical periods or
  immediate erasure of mail-client/backup copies. Article 13(2)(a) permits the
  applicable criteria where specifying a duration is not possible.
- Purposes, conditional contract/legitimate-interest grounds, statutory retention,
  voluntary support information, local report cache and rights are described.
- Dynamic sources use a recipient category in the public text. Article 13(1)(e)
  allows recipients or recipient categories; the category must remain meaningful.
  A fixed provider list is not a substitute for reviewing actual providers.
- The adult instruction in the notice does not implement an age gate or establish
  Families compliance. Child-facing release checks stay in the launch checklist.

## Remaining publication decision

The actual roles and any required international-transfer safeguards for the
download chain and corporate email must be reconciled with the operator's
existing provider/contract records. In particular, an independently operated
download server and a generic warning about US processing do not by themselves
establish a lawful transfer. No universal SCC/DPF coverage, blanket download
consent or transfer exemption has been invented in the candidate. The website
must remain a draft until the applicable classification and disclosures are
settled; simply deleting the old placeholders does not resolve this evidence gap.

The support mailbox product is now confirmed as IONOS Mail Basic. The central
records and website tasks have been informed so that their pending product
question can be closed. This confirmation does not resolve the separate
classification of public media-download providers or approve a new retention
period.

This is the remaining provider/transfer issue already identified in record 017,
not a new request to audit unrelated company processes. The app's final build,
child experience and Store Data safety declaration have separate launch gates.
