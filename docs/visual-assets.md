# Visual assets

The immersive player renders collection backgrounds and optional transparent song motifs as separate layers. `PlaybackVisualCatalog` owns only the dark collection colors and packaged fallback. The catalog and remotely updateable artwork manifest provide independently validated PNG references for the background and song-motif layers. This keeps future artwork data-driven and avoids duplicating player layouts.

`sleepy_bear_moon.png` and `sleepy_lamb_star.png` were generated for this project with OpenAI image generation on 2026-09-09. The collection-specific assets and the later lullaby motifs were generated for this project with OpenAI image generation in September 2026. They contain no third-party source artwork or embedded text and are distributed under the repository's MIT license.

The versioned source PNGs are published in the [artwork-v1 GitHub release](https://github.com/jochenwezel/open-sleep-music/releases/tag/artwork-v1). Every catalog track carries an HTTPS download URL, portable cache filename, and SHA-256 checksum for its artwork. The app downloads and validates artwork independently from audio and falls back silently when an image is unavailable or invalid. Only `sleepy_lamb_star.png` remains packaged as the guaranteed offline fallback; concrete collection and song artwork is not bundled into the APK.

The mutable [artwork-catalog release](https://github.com/jochenwezel/open-sleep-music/releases/tag/artwork-catalog) is the update channel used by installed apps. Its `artwork-catalog.json` can point existing track IDs to newer versioned background and optional song-motif releases and checksums without requiring an application update. The app caches the last valid manifest and falls back to the metadata embedded in its own media catalog when the channel is unavailable or invalid. A manifest entry must supply all three song-motif fields or none of them. Regenerate the manifest with `tools/Export-ArtworkManifest.ps1` after changing artwork assignments.

All five toddler lullabies currently have a separately published song motif. Other collections retain their collection background without a second layer until suitable song motifs have been reviewed and published. Only `sleepy_lamb_star.png` is packaged; it remains the guaranteed offline fallback when a downloaded background or song motif is unavailable.

The starry quiet-classics scene transitions between muted blue and dark blue over 15 seconds per color phase. The fireplace background keeps its subtle three-second opacity crossfade. A visible song motif drifts vertically by only six device-independent pixels over 12 seconds. It is capped at 280 DIP in portrait and 190 DIP in landscape so it stays clear of the compact playback controls. Animation callbacks run at 20 frames per second to limit battery and rendering load, and all ambient animation is stopped with the page and disabled when **Reduce motion** is enabled. Motifs use a strong silhouette, no flashes, and no rapid movement. New artwork should remain readable at phone size in portrait and landscape, use transparent backgrounds, avoid text, and document its source and license here.
